Pre-service consultation

A discussion may be required before an order is confirmed to establish product suitability, vehicle compatibility and any fitting requirements. The products and charges will be confirmed before you place an order.

Dash cams may include a manufacturer's warranty, which may require registration. Warranty information will be provided where applicable. A manufacturer's warranty is additional to your statutory rights.

The full price of the dash cam must be paid before fitting or delivery unless otherwise agreed. Payment may be made by an accepted bank transfer or card payment method. Where equipment is purchased without installation, any delivery charge will be confirmed before the order is placed and is payable before dispatch.

Faulty products will be handled in accordance with the Consumer Rights Act 2015, including applicable rights to a refund, repair or replacement. Please contact ACAMADI Automotive before arranging a return so the appropriate procedure can be confirmed.

Order cancellations

Follow-up appointments

Additional call-outs or follow-up appointments may be chargeable where they fall outside ACAMADI Automotive's obligations to remedy faulty goods or services. Any charge will be discussed and agreed before booking.
