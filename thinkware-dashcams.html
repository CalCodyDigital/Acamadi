Why choose Thinkware?

Protection on the road. And when parked.

Thinkware offers a broad choice of dash cams, from straightforward everyday recording to higher-specification systems. Parking surveillance is a particular strength, with different recording modes and power-saving options across the range.

Best suited to drivers who prioritise parking protection and want a choice of camera setups and recording specifications to suit their needs and budget.

Parking surveillance

Depending on the model, parking options include motion and impact detection, time-lapse recording and energy-saving modes.

Radar on selected models

The U3000 uses built-in radar for its energy-efficient parking mode, helping the camera respond to nearby movement while reducing standby power use.

A wide choice of setups

The ACAMADI range includes front-only and front + rear systems, so you can choose the coverage and recording specification that suit your vehicle.

Choose your coverage

Front-only or front + rear?

Front-only · 1CH

Records the road ahead with a single camera. A straightforward choice if forward-facing footage is your priority.

Front + rear · 2CH

Adds a second camera to record behind the vehicle as well as ahead, giving you more context if an incident happens.

Parking recording needs a suitable power supply. Recording duration depends on the camera settings, power source and vehicle battery. ACAMADI can confirm the features and installation requirements of your chosen model.
