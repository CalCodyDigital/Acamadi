Long-term protection

More protection.
Less maintenance.

A ceramic coating bonds to the vehicle's surface, creating a durable protective layer over the paintwork.

As well as adding gloss, the hydrophobic finish helps repel water and contamination, making the vehicle easier to maintain between washes.

Why ceramic coating?

Protection you can see.

Ceramic coatings offer a more durable alternative to traditional waxes and sealants while improving the appearance and maintainability of the vehicle.

01

Easier maintenance

Hydrophobic behaviour helps water and contamination release more easily from the surface.

02

Paint protection

Adds a protective layer against environmental contamination and chemical exposure.

03

Greater gloss

Enhances depth and reflection for a smoother, more refined-looking finish.

04

Longer lasting

Offers considerably longer-lasting protection than a conventional wax or sealant.

Before the coating

Do I need paint correction first?

Not necessarily.

If you're happy with the condition of the paintwork, preparation and coating may be all your vehicle needs.

If the car has visible swirl marks, haze or other defects, paint enhancement or correction can improve the finish before the coating locks it in.

The coating process

Preparation matters.

01

Inspect

The condition of the vehicle is assessed to determine the preparation required.

02

Prepare

The exterior is thoroughly cleaned and decontaminated before coating.

03

Refine

Where required, the paint is enhanced or corrected before protection is applied.

04

Coat

The selected ceramic coating is applied and allowed to bond and cure correctly.
