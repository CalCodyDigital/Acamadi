More than a car wash

Refinement tailored to your vehicle.

Every vehicle arrives with a different history. Some need a thorough decontamination and protection. Others need machine polishing to restore depth, clarity and gloss.

ACAMADI assesses the condition of your vehicle and recommends the level of detailing needed rather than treating every car the same.

Detailing services

Choose the level of refinement.

From improving tired paintwork to preparing and protecting a new vehicle, the service can be shaped around the condition of your car.

01

Paint enhancement

ACAMADI Enhance

Designed for paintwork that looks dull or has light swirl marks but does not require a full correction.

  • Full safe wash and decontamination
  • Tar and fallout removal
  • Clay treatment where required
  • Single-stage machine polish
  • Paint protection
  • Trim, tyres and exterior finishing
02

Single-stage correction

ACAMADI Gloss

A deeper level of machine polishing for vehicles with more visible swirling, fine scratches and paint haze.

  • Detailed exterior preparation
  • Paint decontamination
  • Precision machine polishing
  • Improved paint clarity
  • Exterior glass treatment
  • Durable finishing protection
03

Protect from the start

New Car Protection

Give a new vehicle the best possible start with careful preparation and protection before everyday use begins to take its toll.

  • Exterior preparation
  • Paint inspection
  • Surface cleansing
  • Protective finishing options
  • Wheels and exterior surfaces
  • Aftercare guidance
04

Interior detailing

Deep Interior Clean

A more intensive interior service for vehicles that need considerably more than a routine valet.

  • Detailed vacuuming
  • Surface-specific cleaning
  • Crevice and detail work
  • Upholstery attention
  • Interior finishing
  • Tailored treatment where required
