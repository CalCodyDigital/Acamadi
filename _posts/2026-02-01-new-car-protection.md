A brand-new car already looks clean and glossy, so adding protection immediately can sound unnecessary.

The point is not to repair a new vehicle. It is to make the finish easier to maintain once the car starts dealing with weather, road grime, insects, bird droppings, washing and everyday family use.

## Exterior protection

Paint protection options such as ceramic coatings create a durable surface designed to improve water behaviour and make routine cleaning easier. Wheels and glass can also be protected depending on the package.

Protection does not make paint invincible, but it can make ongoing maintenance more straightforward.

## What about the interior?

Seats, fabrics and interior trim deal with spills, dirt, clothing dye, pets and frequent contact. Suitable protection can help make some surfaces easier to clean and maintain.

## Does a new car need polishing first?

Not always. New paint can still arrive with light marks or imperfections from transport, storage and preparation, but the required preparation varies from car to car.

A proper inspection should come before deciding how much polishing is necessary.

## Think about how you use the vehicle

A daily family car, work vehicle and weekend car all face different conditions. The right protection package should reflect how the vehicle is actually going to be used rather than simply choosing the longest coating available.
