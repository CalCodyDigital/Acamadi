Winter driving brings shorter daylight hours, more difficult weather and more situations where having a clear record of what happened can be useful.

A dash cam does not make difficult conditions disappear, but it can provide useful footage if an incident happens while driving or while the vehicle is parked.

## Useful evidence after an incident

When visibility is poor or road conditions change quickly, two drivers can remember the same event very differently. Video footage can help show the sequence of events and the conditions at the time.

## Parking protection

Many modern dash cams include parking modes designed to monitor the vehicle while it is stationary. Depending on the camera and installation, this can help capture incidents that happen while you are away from the car.

## Better performance in low light

If you regularly drive early in the morning or after dark, camera quality matters. Resolution, low-light performance and the positioning of the unit all affect the footage you get back.

## A clean installation matters too

A properly routed installation keeps cables out of the way and can make the camera feel much more integrated with the vehicle. Some systems can also be hardwired where appropriate.

Before choosing a camera, think about whether you need front-only coverage, front and rear recording, parking monitoring or remote features. ACAMADI can discuss the options and the fitting requirements for your vehicle.
