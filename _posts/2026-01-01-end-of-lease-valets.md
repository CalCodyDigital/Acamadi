The final few days of a lease can feel busy: mileage, paperwork, handover arrangements and the vehicle inspection all arrive at once.

Cleaning is one of the few parts of that process you can control before the vehicle is handed back.

## Why presentation matters

A heavily soiled vehicle can make it harder to judge its true condition. Stains, brake dust, traffic film and neglected interior areas can also make a car appear less cared for than it really is.

A thorough valet aims to present the vehicle cleanly so its actual condition is easier to assess.

## Areas worth paying attention to

Typical problem areas include carpets and mats, seat staining, pet hair, the boot, dashboard and door trims, wheels, tyres and exterior contamination.

The right treatment depends on how the car has been used and maintained during the lease.

## Don't leave it until the handover morning

Where possible, arrange the valet shortly before the inspection rather than trying to do everything on the day. Remove personal belongings first so the cleaner can access the whole interior properly.

## A tailored service makes more sense

Some vehicles need little more than a thorough clean. Others may benefit from more intensive interior work or additional exterior preparation. ACAMADI can look at the condition of the car and recommend the appropriate level of service rather than applying the same package to every return.
